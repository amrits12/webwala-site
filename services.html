Straight answers

Questions
people ask

If yours isn't here, message me — I'll answer honestly, even if the answer is "you don't need this".

How do payments work?

Half to start, half when the site goes live. For the monthly services (AI assistant and bots) the setup fee is one payment and the monthly fee starts after launch.

You get a written quote first, so you always know the total before paying anything.

What do I need to give you?

Your business name, what you do, your services and prices, and any photos or a logo you already have. If you don't have photos or a logo, tell me — we'll sort out something simple rather than leaving gaps.

Do I have to pay for hosting and a domain separately?

Yes. A domain costs roughly ₹800–1,200 a year and hosting can be free or a few hundred rupees a month depending on the host. I set it up for you and it stays in your name, so you're never locked in to me.

What if I don't like the first design?

You see a design before anything is built out, and we change it until you're happy with the direction. That review round is part of the fixed price — it's not extra billing.

The one thing a fixed price can't cover is a change of plan halfway through, like switching from a five-page site to a full online store. If that happens we re-quote before I continue, never after.

How long does it really take?

Website: 7–10 days. Redesign: 5–7 days. AI assistant or bot: 3–5 days. Those timings assume you send content and feedback quickly — the usual delay on a project is waiting for text and photos, not the building.

Can you update the site after it's live?

Yes. Small text and photo changes are free in the first month (two weeks for a redesign). After that, updates are charged as small fixed jobs — you'll know the price before I touch anything.

Is the AI assistant going to say something wrong to my customers?

It only answers from the business information we load into it, and it hands over to you (WhatsApp or a form) when it doesn't know something. Before launch we test it with the questions your customers actually ask, and you approve its answers.

Do you work outside my city?

Everything happens over call, WhatsApp and email, so location doesn't matter. Most clients I never meet in person.

Not sure what you need?

Call or message me, describe your business in two lines, and I'll tell you honestly what will help — even if that's the cheapest option.
